# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other project spaces is expected to help keep them welcoming and constructive, regardless of background, identity, or experience.

Be respectful, discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people, accept constructive feedback, and respect others' privacy and boundaries. Disagreement is welcome when expressed in good faith. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention or imagery, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

The maintainer is [@syz51](https://github.com/syz51). To request a private reporting channel, open a neutral issue asking how to contact the maintainer privately. Do not include incident details, names of people involved, or other sensitive information in that public request. Share the concern only after a private channel has been arranged. There is no dedicated conduct-reporting inbox or guaranteed response time.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); private vulnerability reports are not a general conduct-reporting channel.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews concerns in context and may request changes, remove or edit harmful contributions, lock conversations, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports will be handled as privately as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Participants may ask the maintainer to reconsider a decision through the arranged private channel.

When adopting this template, replace the maintainer and reporting process with your own verified contact and moderation policy.
